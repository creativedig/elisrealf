Why Trust Us

Transparent. Accountable. Committed to True Impact.

At EL ISRAEL COMFORT HOME AND JOYFUL FOUNDATION, we uphold the highest standards of honesty, transparency, and responsibility in everything we do. We understand that trust is the foundation of every meaningful partnership, especially when it involves the lives of vulnerable children and families. Every donation we receive is treated as a sacred trust. We channel all resources directly into our programs—whether it's feeding and sheltering orphans, supporting children with disabilities, offering professional counseling, or providing quality day care services. We do not divert or misuse funds. Instead, we invest them where they are needed most, ensuring that every contribution leads to real, measurable change. Our operations are guided by clear accountability structures, regular reporting, and an unwavering commitment to ethical practices. We are more than a charity—we are a movement of compassion, integrity, and purposeful impact. When you partner with us, you’re not just giving—you’re joining a mission that is transforming despair into hope, and survival into thriving futures. With us, your generosity becomes action. And your action becomes lasting change.

Our Vision

To create a loving and inclusive society where every child—regardless of background, ability, or circumstance—has the opportunity to grow, thrive, and live with dignity, purpose, and joy.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to providing care, support, and empowerment to vulnerable children through safe housing, early education, disability services, and emotional counseling. Our commitment is rooted in transparency, accountability, and making a lasting impact in the lives of those who need it most.

Compassionate Child Care

We provide a safe, nurturing environment for orphans and vulnerable children. Our home is built on love, dignity, and respect—ensuring every child feels valued, protected, and empowered to grow.

Our counselors offer emotional support, trauma recovery, and family guidance to children and guardians, helping them heal, rebuild confidence, and embrace a hopeful future.

OUR FOUNDER

AINA Oluremi Adedotun

Aina Oluremi Adedotun is a passionate humanitarian, entrepreneur, and advocate for children and women. As the founder of EL ISRAEL COMFORT HOME AND JOYFUL FOUNDATION, she has dedicated her life to bringing comfort, care, and lasting change to vulnerable lives within her community. Her deep concern for the well-being of orphans, children with disabilities, and struggling families has made her a source of hope and strength to many. Aina's personal life is rooted in strong spiritual values and an unshakable commitment to service. She is widely respected for her love for children and her tireless involvement in women’s empowerment and family welfare initiatives. With a solid background in business, Aina has built a successful career as an entrepreneur. Her achievements in the business world gave her the platform and confidence to invest in social causes. Through her leadership, the foundation continues to impact lives and uplift communities—turning compassion into action, and vision into reality.

We believe that serving humanity is the highest calling.
