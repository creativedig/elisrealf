VOLUNTEER

Lend Your Time. Touch a Life. Transform the Future.

At EL ISRAEL COMFORT HOME AND JOYFUL FOUNDATION, we believe that every helping hand makes a difference. Whether you're passionate about caring for children, supporting individuals with disabilities, or lending your skills behind the scenes—there’s a place for you here. Join a community of kind-hearted individuals working together to spread hope, joy, and lasting change.

1. Who can volunteer with your organization?

Anyone with a heart to serve is welcome! Whether you're a student, professional, retiree, or just someone looking to give back—we have opportunities for all ages and skill levels.

2. What kind of volunteer roles are available?

Volunteers can assist in child care, organize donations, support educational activities, help with special needs care, or offer administrative or counseling support, depending on your skills and interests.

3. Do I need prior experience to volunteer?

No prior experience is required! We provide orientation and basic training to help you feel confident and prepared for your role.

4. What kind of volunteer roles are available?

Volunteers can assist in child care, organize donations, support educational activities, help with special needs care, or offer administrative or counseling support, depending on your skills and interests.

5. How can I sign up to volunteer?

Simply fill out our volunteer interest form on the website or contact us directly at: 07030878905,08075861290. Our team will reach out to guide you through the next steps.
