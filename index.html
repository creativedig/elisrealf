Orphan Care & Shelter

Providing a safe, loving, and stable home for orphaned and vulnerable children. We ensure access to education, healthcare, emotional support, and a family environment that nurtures their growth and dreams.

Disability Support Program

Empowering children with disabilities through specialized care, therapy, and inclusive educational support. We advocate for equal opportunities and help each child live a life of dignity and purpose.

Counseling & Emotional Support

Providing professional counseling and emotional care for children and families facing trauma, grief, abuse, or crisis. We walk alongside them with compassion, restoring hope and mental well-being.

Outreach & Support Services

Extending love and relief to families and children in surrounding communities through food drives, school support, health campaigns, and awareness programs. Together, we build stronger, more compassionate neighborhoods.

about us

Transparent. Accountable. Committed to True Impact.

At EL ISRAEL COMFORT HOME AND JOYFUL FOUNDATION, we uphold the highest standards of honesty and responsibility. Every donation, effort, and project is handled with care, integrity, and a deep commitment to transforming lives—not just managing funds. We believe in making a visible difference you can trust.

Our Vision

To create a loving and inclusive society where every child—regardless of background, ability, or circumstance—has the opportunity to grow, thrive, and live with dignity, purpose, and joy.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to providing care, support, and empowerment to vulnerable children through safe housing, early education, disability services, and emotional counseling. Our commitment is rooted in transparency, accountability, and making a lasting impact in the lives of those who need it most.
